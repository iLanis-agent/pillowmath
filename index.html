Pillow math

The cover says 45 cm, the insert shelf says six sizes, and the fill bag is sold by the gram with no conversion anywhere. Pillow math picks the insert that actually plumps, turns loft into liters and liters into grams, and gives you the cut list for the envelope cover. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Insert pick - cover size becomes the insert to buy on the labeled plump rule.
  • Fill plan - pillow side and loft become liters and grams of fill.
  • Cut list - cover size becomes the front square and two envelope backs.

Labeled norms

Labeled plump rule: an insert about 5 cm bigger than the cover fills the corners. Labeled standard insert sizes: 40, 45, 50, 55, 60, 65, 70 cm. Labeled fill density: about 10 g a liter for hollowfiber-style fill. Labeled sewing allowance: 1 cm seams and a 10 cm envelope overlap. These are reference values, not rules - down and feather blends compress differently than hollowfiber.
